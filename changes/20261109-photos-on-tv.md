Summary: Photos on a TV
Details: Photos appears on the TV home, with each day as a row you walk with the remote. Photographs you can't reach right now stay in place, dimmed and marked with where they are. Albums, places and every source are on a rail above. The viewer shows the whole photograph full screen, OK marks a favourite, and a slideshow skips what it can't open and says how many first. There is no delete or editing on a TV.
