Summary: A Volume mixer built around what's playing
Details: Sound's Volume mixer lists each app that is playing as its song or video, with its artwork, play and skip buttons, a volume slider and a live level. Send one app to other speakers or headphones from its row, such as a music app to the living room while everything else stays on your speakers. Pick where the rest of your sound goes from the tiles at the top, and set system sounds and the ringtone under Other sounds.
