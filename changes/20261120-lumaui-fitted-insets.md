Summary: Insets fit their content, and editing from the action bar keeps the selection's name
Details: A dock inset such as a mixer is as tall as what it holds, so no panel is padded with empty space, and on a phone it stays in view under the main view. Growing the action bar into an editor no longer squeezes the selected item's name, inset headers are shorter and symmetric, and a row's trailing switch can wait for hover while an off state stays visible.
