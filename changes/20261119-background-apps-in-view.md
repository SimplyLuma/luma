Summary: Background apps stay in view
Details: Apps running in the background now sit in the status area while there is room. When there are more than fit, the rest fold into one button that shows their icons and how many there are, and it never hides a single app. A web app installed from a browser now shows its own name and icon, and its notifications are its own, instead of the browser's.
