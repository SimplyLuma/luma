Summary: The TV home's focus rings are smooth and Home returns to where you were
Details: Focus rings on app tiles, the app switcher, the search field and system dialog fields now have smooth, evenly rounded corners. An open app shows a soft running light under its name instead of a dot, and apps waiting in the background no longer count as open. Pressing Home in an app returns to the page it was opened from, closing the last app no longer leaves a black screen, Android apps without a TV tile show a large icon on their own colour, and Android apps that open in a small window go full screen.
