Summary: Choose every widget's source; edits undo
Details: Each widget's bar starts with its source: pick a camera, its resolution and frame rate, a microphone or an output, or share another display or window. Problems say what went wrong, edits undo with Ctrl+Z, and a widget without a picture shows a card in its place.
