Summary: Streamy remembers what you shared and hears the apps you choose
Details: A display or window you shared comes back without asking again, and if it can't, Streamy asks once and says what happened. Add App audio to carry the sound of the apps you pick. The audio strips take only the room they need, and each widget's row shows at a glance whether it is hidden or muted.
