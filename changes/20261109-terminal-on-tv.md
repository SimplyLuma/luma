Summary: Terminal on a TV
Details: Terminal appears on the TV home and opens straight to your shell, large enough to read across the room, on your wallpaper. Type with the TV keyboard or a keyboard plugged into the TV. The bar at the bottom switches sessions, starts a new one and has the keys a remote lacks: esc, tab, Ctrl+C and the arrows. Back goes from the shell to the bar, and from the bar to the TV home.
