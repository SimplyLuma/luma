Summary: Terminal on a TV
Details: Terminal appears on the TV home and shows a session large enough to read across the room, driven by the remote. It runs commands saved with Save for TV on a computer and cannot type. Left and Right jump between lines that report a failure, Compare shows two sessions side by side, it starts locked, and stopping a session needs OK held.
