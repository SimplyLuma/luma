Summary: Streamy, for streaming and recording
Details: A new app puts displays, windows, cameras, images and sound together in scenes, with rounded corners, a stroke and a soft shadow on any picture. Arrange widgets by hand on a landscape or vertical canvas, then go live or record to a file. Only the scene on screen captures, and nothing runs while you aren't using it.
