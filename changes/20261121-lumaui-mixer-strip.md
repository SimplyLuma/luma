Summary: Mixer strips for audio panels
Details: Apps can show each audio source as a channel strip, with its name and device, a level meter with peak hold, a volume fader with a dB scale, and Mute and Monitor buttons. Strips sit side by side and scroll sideways when they don't all fit. Double-click a fader to return to 0 dB, scroll or use the arrow keys to adjust it, and press M to mute.
