Summary: Apps update together with Luma
Details: When a Luma update downloads, the updates waiting for your apps download with it and are installed during the restart, before you sign in. Updates that ask for more permissions still wait for you to review them in Depot. When app updates are waiting at sign-in, they start straight away.
