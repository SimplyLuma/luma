Summary: Built-in apps stay up to date
Details: Luma's built-in apps and their runtimes now update in the background every six hours and soon after you sign in, without waiting for a Luma update and a restart. Apps that are open keep their current version until you open them again. Updates that ask for more permissions still wait for you in Depot, and updates wait on metered connections, in power saving and on a low battery.
