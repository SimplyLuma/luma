Summary: TV apps open in about half a second
Details: On a TV, the apps on the TV home are loaded in the background after sign-in, one at a time, so opening one shows it at once instead of starting it. Tide opened in 0.5 seconds on a Raspberry Pi 5, down from 4 to 6 seconds. Nothing appears on screen until you open an app, and a closed app is made ready again.
