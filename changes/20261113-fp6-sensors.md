Summary: Auto-rotate and automatic brightness on the Fairphone 6
Details: The Fairphone 6 kernel drives its accelerometer, gyroscope, light, proximity, compass and pressure sensors, so the screen turns with the phone and brightness follows the room. Needs the new boot image and kernel-modules-luma-fp6 7.1.2-1.luma.ims1.gmu2.selinux1.3.
