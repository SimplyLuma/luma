Summary: One Sound page for outputs and what's playing
Details: The Sound page in Quick Options now shows your outputs as buttons you switch with one tap, and a row for each app that is playing, with its artwork, title, volume and a play or pause button. Apps that aren't playing are no longer listed. A link opens the full Volume mixer in Settings.
