Summary: Installed apps are kept to themselves
Details: Apps installed from .deb, .rpm and AppImage files now see only their own saved passwords, can no longer record the microphone or the screen behind your back, cannot read or control other apps through accessibility, and no longer hold the key to other apps' windows. Sign-ins you already have carry over.
