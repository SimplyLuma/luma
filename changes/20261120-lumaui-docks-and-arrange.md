Summary: Movable panels, a canvas you arrange by hand, and drawn level meters
Details: Creative apps can dock any number of panels on the left, the right or the bottom, move them by dragging or from their menu, and save the arrangement as a layout. A new arrange view lets people move and resize things on a canvas with handles, snapping guides and the arrow keys. Level meters are drawn by LumaUI with peak hold, and a live chip in the action bar can show a stream's bitrate beside its running time.
