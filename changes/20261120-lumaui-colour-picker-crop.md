Summary: A colour picker, and crop and resize from the centre in creative apps
Details: Colour swatches open a full picker: a colour area with hue and opacity, Luma's palette, the document's colours, your recent colours, an eyedropper for anything on screen, and a hex code. Changes preview as you drag and count as one step to undo. On a canvas, dragging a handle keeps a picture's proportions; hold Alt to resize from the centre, Shift to stretch, or Ctrl to crop that edge, and double-click a picture to crop it until you press Enter or Esc.
