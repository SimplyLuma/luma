Summary: TV Mode for the living room
Details: Choose TV in Quick Options to use Luma from the sofa with a remote or gamepad: a TV home with apps and search, a TV keyboard, system dialogs and a quick panel sized for the screen, and Settings, Connect, Tide, Weather and Depot laid out for the remote. Desktop, tablet and mobile are unchanged.
