Summary: Drag panels anywhere, side by side, stacked or as tabs
Details: Drag a panel by its header to the side of another to split it, onto its middle to stack them as tabs, or to the window's edge for a full-height column or full-width row. A preview shows exactly where it will land before you let go, and Esc cancels.
