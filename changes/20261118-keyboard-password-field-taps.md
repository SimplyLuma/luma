Summary: Tapping the password field opens the keyboard on phones
Details: On the login and lock screens of a phone or tablet, a tap anywhere on the password field now opens the on-screen keyboard, and tapping the field again keeps it open. A tap below the text line did nothing, and a second tap closed the keyboard.
