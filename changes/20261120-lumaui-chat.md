Summary: A live chat part for apps that show a stream's chat
Details: Chat lines show each person's name in their own colour, adjusted so it stays readable in light and dark, with small badges for broadcasters, moderators and others. The chat keeps up with new messages and offers a button back to the newest when you scroll up.
