Summary: TV apps keep their sidebar and gain an action bar
Details: In TV Mode an app's sidebar stays open beside the page and fades at its edges as it scrolls. Apps with an action bar get it on the remote's menu key, with chips for choices and search that lands on the results. Sandboxed apps show your wallpaper behind them instead of black.
