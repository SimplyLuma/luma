Summary: Right-click menus open where you click, with submenus
Details: Menus opened with a right click or a long press appear at the pointer and flip to stay on screen instead of jumping to the edge of what you clicked. Menus can hold submenus that open as you point at them, stay open while you move toward them, and work with the arrow keys and Esc; on a phone they open in place with a Back row. Shortcuts show beside menu items, and dragging an inset by its header shows where it will land again.
