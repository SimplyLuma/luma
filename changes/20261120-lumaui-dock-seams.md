Summary: Inset seams can be dragged anywhere, and keep their size
Details: A seam between insets moves anywhere between their minimum sizes, including next to an inset that fits its content; the size you drag to is kept with the layout, and a double click on the seam fits the inset to its content again. Seams show the resize pointer, and a seam that cannot move shows neither the pointer nor its mark.
