Summary: Select, move and resize several things on a canvas at once
Details: Shift-click to add things to a selection, drag across empty canvas to select everything you touch, or press Ctrl+A. Several selected things share one frame: drag to move them together, pull its handles to resize them together, and nudge them with the arrow keys.
