Summary: Depot on a TV, with every place
Details: Depot on a TV is now the redesigned Depot, with the places it has on a computer, tablet and phone: Today, Apps, Looks, Tweaks, Library, Updates and Search. It lists what runs on a TV and says whether an app is driven with the remote, a controller or a keyboard. It also switches to its TV layout when the TV posture arrives after Depot has started.
