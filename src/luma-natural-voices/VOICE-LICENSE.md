# LJSpeech medium voice: public domain

Bryce Beattie, the model author, states on his Piper voice download page that
all listed models are placed in the public domain and can be used commercially
or noncommercially and reuploaded. The medium model is distributed directly
from the author at https://brycebeattie.com/files/tts/.

The source LJSpeech dataset is public domain, as documented by its publisher
at https://keithito.com/LJ-Speech-Dataset/. Its original LibriVox recordings
are public domain as well.
